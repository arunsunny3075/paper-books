Rules in force

Signalmomentum blended over 63/126/252 days, skipping the last 42 days so a fresh spike is never chased
Universetop 300 by market cap plus gold, silver, Nasdaq-100, Nifty, Bank and IT ETFs, ranked together
Bookslive at 21d, slow at 42d, fast at 10d, seg63 at 63d, seg42 at 42d, seg21 at 21d, seg10 at 10d, seg63x at 63d, nano63 at 63d — independent state, orders, journal and equity curve each
Holdingstop 3, equal weight, held untouched between rebalances — every stop-loss rule tested made the result worse
Filtersno trend filter — it cost return without cutting the drawdown
Sizingequal weight, capped at 40% per name
Neverno leverage · no shorting · no options · no borrowed money

Re-measured 10-Aug-2026. The earlier 62.4% / −32.2% does not reproduce; the same code gives 43.5% / −48.3%. The rule beats chance decisively — 60 of 60 matched random rotations — but every figure comes from a universe picked by 2026 market cap in which no company has ever delisted. Plan on 15–20%, and on a drawdown near 40%. The books exist to test the engine at several cadences, not to settle which cadence is best: over a one-year window the better cadence finishes ahead only about half the time.
